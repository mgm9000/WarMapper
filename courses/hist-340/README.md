2. 9 Jan reads "chapter 1 (19-43)"; in the 2009 volume 1 those pages are chapter 2.
3. 14 Jan gives the Hancock letter pages as "393-40" (the end page looks cut off).
4. The second paper (6 Mar) is noted under 27 Feb; 6 Mar is also a class day.
5. 22 Jan (first paper) is not a class day.
