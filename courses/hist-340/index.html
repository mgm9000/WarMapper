Course guide

HIST 340
U.S. Military History

A battlefield study guide for service members and students: each class day lists its readings and, as they are built, the battle playbacks that go with it, on maps drawn at the time and on today’s ground.

Term dates are not yet known. Every date is a placeholder marked “update”. Class days are numbered, and meet on Tuesday and Thursday, 08:30–09:50.

Readings are text and links only: nothing copyrighted is hosted here. Readings on Canvas or in books are named with no link.
